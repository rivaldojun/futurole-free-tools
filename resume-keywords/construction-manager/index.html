Common mistakes on Construction Manager resumes

  • Listing duties instead of results, such as 'managed site' without specifying budget size or timeline impact.
  • Omitting specific software names like Procore or Primavera P6, which are critical for ATS filtering.
  • Failing to mention safety certifications like OSHA 30, a non-negotiable requirement for most roles.
  • Using vague terms like 'good communication' instead of citing specific stakeholder management examples.

FAQ

What are the top ATS keywords for Construction Manager roles?

Prioritize terms like CPM scheduling, OSHA compliance, Procore, and cost control. These specific technical terms signal immediate operational capability to automated screening systems.

Should I list software skills on a Construction Manager resume?

Yes, include specific platforms like Procore, Primavera P6, or Microsoft Project. Recruiters use these tools to verify your ability to manage digital workflows and documentation.

How do I quantify experience for a Construction Manager resume?

Use project value, team size, and schedule variance. State the total contract value, number of subcontractors managed, and percentage of budget saved or time gained.

Is OSHA 30 certification required for Construction Managers?

Most US employers require OSHA 30 or equivalent safety training. Listing this certification demonstrates compliance knowledge and reduces liability concerns for hiring managers.

What is the best format for a Construction Manager resume?

Use a reverse-chronological format with a clear skills section. This structure allows recruiters to quickly verify recent project experience and technical proficiency.

How important is LEED certification for this role?

LEED certification is valuable for green building projects but not mandatory for all roles. Include it if you have worked on sustainable projects to highlight specialized expertise.
