FAQ

What keywords should I include in a Paralegal resume?

Include terms like litigation support, legal research, e‑discovery, case management, document drafting, and compliance. Place them in the summary, skills, and experience sections.

How many years of experience do recruiters expect for a Paralegal role?

Recruiters look for relevant experience rather than a specific number of years. Highlight achievements that show competence in core paralegal tasks.

Should I list software I have used on my Paralegal resume?

Yes. Name platforms such as Westlaw, LexisNexis, Clio, MyCase, and Relativity. ATS scans for these exact tools.

Is a certification necessary for a Paralegal resume?

Certifications are not mandatory but they strengthen your profile. Common ones include CP, ACP, and NCP.

How can I avoid common resume mistakes as a Paralegal?

Focus on action verbs, quantify results, and tailor each bullet to the job description. Remove unrelated duties.

What format works best for ATS-friendly Paralegal resumes?

Use a simple chronological layout, clear headings, and standard fonts. Avoid tables, graphics, and complex formatting.
