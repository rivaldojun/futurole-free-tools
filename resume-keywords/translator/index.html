FAQ

What keywords should I include in my Translator resume?

Insert terms like multilingual proficiency, CAT tools, localization, proofreading, and industry‑specific vocabularies. Place them in your headline, summary, and skills sections to match ATS filters.

How do I showcase language proficiency effectively?

State each language with a clear proficiency level, such as native, fluent, or professional. Pair the level with relevant translation domains you have handled.

Which CAT tools are most valued by recruiters?

Tools such as SDL Trados, MemoQ, and Wordfast appear frequently in job descriptions. Mention the specific versions you have mastered.

Should I list translation specialties?

Yes. Highlight areas like legal, medical, technical, or marketing translation. This signals fit for niche roles and improves keyword density.

How can I demonstrate measurable impact without numbers?

Describe outcomes like improved document clarity, reduced revisions, faster turnaround, or higher client satisfaction, using placeholders if needed.

What common resume mistakes should I avoid as a translator?

Avoid vague language, passive phrasing, and generic skill lists. Focus on concrete actions, tools used, and results achieved.
