FAQ

How important is it to include keywords from the job description in my resume?

Including relevant keywords from the job description is crucial as ATS systems scan resumes for these keywords to match candidates with job openings. It also helps recruiters quickly identify qualified candidates.

Should I include a cover letter with my resume?

While not always required, a well-crafted cover letter can help you stand out and provide an opportunity to highlight your qualifications and enthusiasm for the role. It's a good idea to include one when applying for jobs.

How often should I update my resume?

It's a good practice to update your resume whenever you gain new skills, complete a significant project, or change jobs. This ensures your resume always reflects your current qualifications and experience.
