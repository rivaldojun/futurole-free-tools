Common mistakes on Human Resources Manager resumes

  • Lack of specific keywords related to HR and ATS systems.
  • Overemphasis on generic skills without quantifiable achievements.
  • Inclusion of irrelevant certifications or degrees.
  • Use of passive voice and vague language that does not highlight achievements.

FAQ

How important are certifications for a Human Resources Manager resume?

Certifications can boost your resume, but they are not mandatory. Focus on relevant, industry-recognized certifications like SHRM-CP or PHR. Highlight any certifications in your resume summary or skills section.

Should I include volunteer work or internships on my resume?

Yes, include volunteer work or internships that demonstrate relevant skills and experience. Tailor these experiences to highlight transferable skills like communication, teamwork, and problem-solving.

How can I make my resume ATS-friendly?
