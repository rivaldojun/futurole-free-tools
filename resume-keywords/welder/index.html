Resume bullet examples for a Welder

  • Completed [X] TIG welds on stainless steel components to [X] dimensional specifications.
  • Interpreted weld symbols and shop drawings to select the correct joint preparation and welding process.
  • Performed MIG and flux-cored arc welding on [X] carbon steel assemblies, meeting [X] quality requirements.
  • Set up welding equipment, selected wire and filler rod, and adjusted parameters to achieve compliant weld penetration.
  • Inspected welds for porosity, undercut, and incomplete fusion, then reworked defects to meet drawing requirements.
  • Positioned and secured parts using jigs and clamps to limit distortion during welding and fit-up.

Common mistakes on Welder resumes

  • Listing only equipment names without naming the welding process, base material, or joint type.
  • Leaving out certifications, weld symbols, blueprint reading, and inspection methods that the job posting requires.
  • Describing duties without showing the outcome, such as stating that you welded parts without naming the material or standard.
  • Using a summary that repeats the job title instead of showing process, material, and equipment experience.

FAQ

What are the most important keywords for a Welder resume?

Include the welding processes you have used, such as TIG, MIG, FCAW, and stick welding, plus the materials you weld, such as carbon steel, stainless steel, or aluminum. Add blueprint reading, weld symbol interpretation, inspection methods, equipment setup, and any certifications that match the job posting.

How do I write welder resume bullets that show my skill?

Name the process, material, and task in each bullet, then state the result. For example: "Completed TIG welds on stainless steel components to [X] dimensional specifications." Replace the brackets with details from your own work.

Should a welder resume include certifications?

Include certifications when the employer asks for them or when they prove a process qualification. Common examples include AWS Certified Welder, AWS Certified Welding Inspector, and ASME Section IX WPS/PQR credentials. List the issuing body, credential name, and date so the hiring manager can verify them.

How do I get past ATS screening for a welder job?

Mirror the job posting's wording for welding processes, materials, inspection methods, and required credentials. Keep the process names in your skills section and repeat the relevant ones in your bullet points. Check that your file is a readable document and that your contact details sit at the top.

What skills should I list if I have limited welding experience?

List the processes you have practiced, the materials you have handled, and the shop tasks you can perform, such as fit-up, grinding, or material handling. Add blueprint reading, safety training, and certifications you hold. Describe your experience accurately and avoid claiming processes you have not used.

How do I tailor a welder resume to a specific job?

Read the posting and mark every process, material, equipment, and credential it names. Match your wording to those terms, then reorder your bullets so the most relevant work appears first.
