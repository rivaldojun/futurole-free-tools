Common mistakes on Mechanical Engineer resumes

  • Listing duties without quantifiable impact
  • Omitting specific software versions or tools
  • Using vague terms like "responsible for design" instead of concrete actions
  • Formatting that confuses ATS parsers

FAQ

What keywords should I include for a Mechanical Engineer resume?

Include software names, analysis methods, and industry standards that appear in the job posting. Match terms such as SolidWorks, FEA, GD&T, and project management.

How many years of experience should I list?

State the total years in the field and highlight relevant experience for the target role. Use a concise timeline that aligns with the job requirements.

Should I add a summary section?

A brief summary that mentions core competencies and key achievements helps recruiters and ATS quickly see fit for the position.
