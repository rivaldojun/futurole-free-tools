Common mistakes on Communications Manager resumes

  • Listing tactical tasks like 'wrote press releases' without showing strategic impact or business outcomes
  • Omitting crisis communication experience or describing it vaguely without scale, speed, or resolution details
  • Failing to quantify audience reach, sentiment shift, media value, or engagement metrics for campaigns
  • Using generic phrases like 'excellent communication skills' instead of specifying channels, audiences, and objectives managed

FAQ

What are the most important Communications Manager resume keywords for ATS?

ATS systems prioritize strategic communications planning, media relations, crisis management, content strategy, stakeholder communication, and measurement analytics. Include specific tools like Cision, Meltwater, Google Analytics, and CMS platforms. Pair each keyword with a quantified result to pass both automated and human review.

How do I show crisis communication experience on a Communications Manager resume?

Describe the crisis type, your role in the response team, actions taken within the first hour, media outlets managed, message consistency across channels, and the outcome measured by sentiment recovery time, coverage volume reduction, or stakeholder trust metrics. Name the framework used if applicable.

Should I include internal communications on a Communications Manager resume?

Yes. Internal communications is a core function for most Communications Manager roles. Highlight employee engagement initiatives, change management communications, executive messaging, channel strategy (intranet, Slack, town halls), and measurable improvements in engagement surveys or adoption rates.

What metrics matter most on a Communications Manager resume?

Recruiters look for earned media value, share of voice, sentiment scores, media placement tier quality, audience reach and growth, engagement rates, employee engagement survey improvements, message penetration rates, and communications ROI. Always pair a metric with the business objective it supported.

How do I tailor a Communications Manager resume for corporate vs agency roles?

For corporate roles, emphasize stakeholder management, executive communications, internal communications, investor relations, and cross-functional leadership. For agency roles, highlight multi-client portfolio management, new business pitches, media relations volume, crisis simulation experience, and billable hour efficiency or revenue growth.

Which certifications help a Communications Manager resume stand out?

The APR (Accredited in Public Relations) is the most recognized credential. CCP (Certified Communications Professional) adds value for strategic roles. PMP demonstrates project leadership. HubSpot and Google Analytics certifications prove digital measurement competence. List only current, relevant certifications.
