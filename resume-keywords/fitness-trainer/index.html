FAQ

Which certifications are most valued on a fitness trainer resume?

NASM, ACE, and ACSM are widely recognized standards in the US market. CSCS is the gold standard for those targeting athletic or strength and conditioning roles.

How do I show results if I do not have exact percentages?

Describe the specific change in a client's ability or health. Mention moving from a sedentary state to completing a specific race or lifting a target weight.

Should I include nutrition advice on my resume?

Include nutritional guidance if you have the certification to provide it. Limit this to general guidelines to stay within the legal scope of practice for trainers.

How do I handle a gap in my training experience?

List any continuing education credits or workshops completed during the gap. Focus on the skills you maintained or acquired through personal practice.

Do I need to list every piece of equipment I can use?

List specialized equipment like TRX, Kettlebells, or Pilates reformers. Standard gym equipment is assumed and does not need a dedicated list.
