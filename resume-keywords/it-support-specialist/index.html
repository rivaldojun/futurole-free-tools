Resume bullet examples for a IT Support Specialist

  • Resolved [X] hardware, software, and network tickets per day while sustaining [X]% first-call resolution across Windows and macOS endpoints.
  • Administered Active Directory and Microsoft 365 accounts for [X] users, handling provisioning, permissions, and offboarding with zero security incidents.
  • Deployed and imaged [X] workstations using SCCM and Intune, cutting average setup time for new hires.
  • Troubleshot VPN, Wi-Fi, and DNS connectivity issues for remote staff, restoring access within SLA targets on [X]% of incidents.
  • Documented [X] knowledge base articles that reduced repeat tickets on common Outlook and printer problems.
  • Managed ServiceNow queue of [X] daily incidents and service requests while meeting or exceeding team SLA compliance rates.

Common mistakes on IT Support Specialist resumes

  • Listing only generic duties such as "fixed computers" or "helped users" without naming tools, ticket volume, or measurable outcomes.
  • Omitting specific platforms like ServiceNow, Active Directory, or Windows 10/11 that ATS systems and recruiters filter for.
  • Including outdated technologies such as Windows 7 or retired imaging tools that signal skills have not kept pace.
  • Failing to show customer-facing results like first-call resolution, SLA adherence, or user satisfaction impact.

FAQ

What keywords should I put on an IT Support Specialist resume for ATS?

Include Active Directory, ServiceNow or Jira Service Management, Windows 10/11, Microsoft 365, hardware troubleshooting, network diagnostics (TCP/IP, DNS, VPN), remote support tools, and SLA or first-call resolution language. Mirror the exact terms from the job posting and prove each one in an experience bullet.

Which certifications help an IT Support Specialist resume the most?

CompTIA A+ remains the most common baseline for entry and mid-level roles. CompTIA Network+, ITIL Foundation, and Microsoft 365 Modern Desktop Administrator certifications appear frequently in U.S. postings and strengthen both ATS matching and recruiter confidence.

How do I quantify achievements on an IT Support Specialist resume?

Use ticket volume, first-call resolution rate, SLA compliance percentage, number of users or devices supported, and time saved on imaging or onboarding. Replace vague statements with concrete results such as "resolved [X] tickets daily while holding [X]% first-call resolution."

Should soft skills appear on an IT Support Specialist resume?

Yes. Place patient communication, clear explanation to non-technical users, prioritization, and empathy in a short skills section or weave them into bullets. Recruiters value evidence that you can calm frustrated users and document solutions clearly.

How long should an IT Support Specialist resume be?

Keep it to one page if you have fewer than five years of experience. Two pages work for candidates with longer tenure or additional leadership responsibilities, provided every line shows relevant tools, scope, or measurable results.

What is the biggest mistake on IT Support Specialist resumes?

Writing duty lists without tools or metrics. Statements such as "provided technical support" fail both ATS keyword scans and hiring managers. Name the platforms you used and the outcomes you delivered instead.
