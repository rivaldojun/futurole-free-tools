Common mistakes on Plumber resumes

  • Listing tools without mentioning the specific tasks they performed.
  • Omitting the specific type of plumbing experience like residential or commercial.
  • Ignoring local plumbing codes and licensure details.
  • Using vague terms like fixed pipes instead of technical methods.

FAQ

Which certifications help a plumber resume stand out?

A Journeyman or Master Plumber license proves your professional standing. OSHA 10 certification shows you follow safety standards on job sites.

Should I list every tool I use on my resume?

List tools that relate to the specific job description. Focus on specialized equipment like hydro-jetters or pipe threaders to show expertise.

How do I show my experience with plumbing codes?

Mention the specific codes you follow, such as the Uniform Plumbing Code. Describe a project where you ensured full compliance during an inspection.

Do I need to specify residential or commercial experience?

Yes. Recruiters search for these specific terms to match you with the right project type.

How can I quantify my plumbing achievements?

Use numbers to show the volume of installations or repairs you completed. Mention the size of the buildings or the number of units you serviced.

Is a summary section necessary for a plumber?

A summary highlights your years of experience and primary specializations. It gives the recruiter a quick snapshot of your technical level.
