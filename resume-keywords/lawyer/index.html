Common mistakes on Lawyer resumes

  • Listing only practice areas without matter types, tools, or outcomes
  • Using generic phrases like 'legal work' instead of searchable skills
  • Omitting bar admissions, jurisdictions, and court admissions
  • Copying job description keywords without matching them to experience

FAQ

What keywords should a Lawyer put on a resume?

Match the posting's practice area, matter types, tools, and outcomes. Include legal research platforms, drafting, litigation or transactional tasks, compliance, and client work that you have actually performed.

How should I use Lawyer resume keywords without keyword stuffing?

Place them in context within experience bullets, skills, and summary. Pair each keyword with a matter, client type, or result so ATS and recruiters see evidence.

Do Lawyer resumes need bar admissions and court admissions?

Yes, list active bar admissions, jurisdictions, and any federal or specialty court admissions near the top. Include license status and number if the posting requests it.

What ATS keywords do law firms and legal departments search for?

They often search for practice-area terms, document types, research tools, e-discovery, compliance frameworks, and matter-management software named in the job description.

How many keywords should a Lawyer resume include?

There is no fixed number. Include the relevant terms you can support with experience, usually concentrated in a skills section and woven into bullets.
