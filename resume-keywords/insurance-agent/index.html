Common mistakes on Insurance Agent resumes

  • Listing license numbers or expiration dates instead of active lines of authority
  • Omitting specific carrier appointments and product lines you are authorized to sell
  • Using generic sales language without naming policy types (auto, home, commercial, life)
  • Failing to mention continuing education compliance or state regulatory knowledge

FAQ

What are the most important Insurance Agent resume keywords for ATS?

ATS systems prioritize active license types (Property & Casualty, Life & Health), specific carrier appointments, CRM platforms like Applied Epic or Salesforce, and regulatory compliance terms. Include exact policy lines you sell such as commercial auto, workers compensation, or term life.

Should I list my insurance license number on my resume?

Do not include your license number or expiration date on a public resume. Instead, state your active lines of authority, resident state, and non-resident licenses held. Recruiters verify licenses through NIPR or state databases after initial screening.

How do I show sales performance without violating confidentiality?

Use percentages, rankings, or relative metrics such as 'top 10% of agency producers' or 'exceeded renewal targets by [X]%'. Avoid naming specific premium volumes or client names. Focus on retention rates, cross-sell ratios, and new business growth percentages.

Which certifications do hiring managers value most for Insurance Agents?

CPCU and CIC are the most recognized designations for P&C agents. CLU and LUTCF carry weight for life and health producers. AIC is valuable for claims-focused roles. List designations in progress with expected completion dates.

How should I format carrier appointments on my resume?

Create a dedicated 'Carrier Appointments' section listing each carrier and the specific lines of authority granted (e.g., 'Travelers: Commercial Package, Workers Comp, Commercial Auto'). This proves product breadth better than a generic 'multiple carrier relationships' statement.

What technology skills should an Insurance Agent highlight?

Prioritize agency management systems (Applied Epic, Vertafore, AMS360, EZLynx), CRM platforms (Salesforce, HubSpot, AgencyBloc), and comparative raters (PL Rating, TurboRater). Mention e-signature tools, document management, and any insurtech platforms you have used.
