Common mistakes on Copywriter resumes

  • Using generic phrases instead of specific writing or editing skills
  • Omitting measurable outcomes or results from past projects
  • Listing unrelated skills without showing how they apply to copywriting
  • Ignoring ATS by not including relevant keywords from the job description

FAQ

What keywords should I use for a copywriter resume?

Use keywords like content writing, SEO, editing, storytelling, brand voice, and digital marketing tools. These show your relevant skills and help pass ATS filters.

How many keywords are enough in a copywriter resume?

Include enough keywords to demonstrate your skills clearly, typically 15 to 20 relevant terms tied to the job description and industry standards.

Should I include tools like Google Analytics or CMS in my copywriter resume?

Yes, mentioning tools like Google Analytics and CMS shows your technical proficiency and ability to manage content effectively.

How do I show creativity in a copywriter resume?

Highlight creative storytelling, content development, and examples of campaigns where your writing made a measurable impact.

Is it important to quantify achievements on a copywriter resume?

Yes, quantifying achievements with metrics like engagement rates or traffic growth makes your impact clear and credible.

Are certifications important for a copywriter resume?

Certifications like SEO or content marketing can strengthen your resume by proving your expertise and commitment to professional growth.

How can I tailor my resume for ATS when applying as a copywriter?

Use keywords from the job posting, match your skills to the role, include standard job titles, and avoid unusual fonts or graphics.
