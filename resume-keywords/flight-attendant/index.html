Resume bullet examples for a Flight Attendant

  • Executed emergency evacuation drill for [X] passengers within 90-second FAA standard, coordinating slide deployment and crowd control.
  • Administered CPR and AED to passenger in cardiac arrest at 35,000 ft, stabilizing condition until ground EMS transfer.
  • De-escalated intoxicated passenger disturbance using verbal compliance techniques, avoiding diversion and ensuring cabin safety.
  • Managed bilingual announcements and special assistance for [X] non-English speaking passengers during irregular operations.
  • Identified undeclared hazardous material in carry-on baggage during pre-flight scan, preventing regulatory violation.
  • Recovered service failure for premium cabin by reallocating inventory and coordinating with purser, retaining customer loyalty.

Common mistakes on Flight Attendant resumes

  • Listing generic 'customer service' without aviation-specific examples like evacuation or medical response.
  • Omitting FAA Certificate of Demonstrated Proficiency or recency dates.
  • Using military time or airline codes without explaining relevance to civilian operations.
  • Including full-body photos or physical stats unless explicitly requested by the carrier.

FAQ

What are the most important Flight Attendant resume keywords for ATS?

ATS prioritizes FAA Certificate of Demonstrated Proficiency, emergency evacuation, door operation, first aid/CPR/AED, dangerous goods, CRM, and specific aircraft types. Include these exact terms in a core competencies section.

Should I list specific aircraft types on my Flight Attendant resume?

Yes. List every aircraft type you are qualified on (e.g., B737, A320, B777). Recruiters filter for current qualifications to minimize training costs and determine immediate line eligibility.

How do I show language skills on a Flight Attendant resume?

Create a dedicated Languages section. List languages with proficiency levels (ILR or CEFR) and note 'Language Qualified' or 'Language of Destination' status if certified by a carrier for premium routes.

What is the biggest mistake on a Flight Attendant resume?

Treating the role as hospitality-only. Airlines hire safety professionals first. Lead with emergency procedures, regulatory compliance, and safety equipment mastery before service achievements.

Do I need a cover letter for a Flight Attendant application?

Major US carriers often require a cover letter answering specific behavioral questions (STAR format). Address the airline's values, base preferences, and willingness to relocate or work reserve schedules.

How far back should a Flight Attendant resume go?

Include the last 10-15 years of flying experience. Prior non-aviation roles only need one line each unless they demonstrate transferable safety, medical, or leadership authority relevant to the cabin.
