Common mistakes on Social Worker resumes

  • Overusing social work jargon without explaining impact.
  • Failing to quantify achievements or outcomes.
  • Neglecting to highlight specific populations served.
  • Omitting relevant certifications or licenses.

FAQ

What certifications should I include on a Social Worker resume?

Include licenses like LCSW or LMSW, and certifications such as C-SWCM or CTP, especially if required by the job posting.

How do I show empathy on a resume?

Highlight client-centered achievements, such as improving engagement or reducing crisis incidents, to demonstrate empathetic practice.

Should I list volunteer experience on a Social Worker resume?

Yes, include relevant volunteer work if it demonstrates skills like case management or community advocacy.

How important is cultural competence in a Social Worker resume?

Cultural competence is critical; mention specific experiences or training that show your ability to work with diverse populations.

What's the best way to describe case management experience?

Use action verbs like 'coordinated,' 'developed,' or 'managed,' and quantify outcomes, such as caseload size or successful discharges.

Do I need to include EHR experience on my resume?

Yes, list EHR systems you're familiar with, as many agencies use them for client documentation and management.
