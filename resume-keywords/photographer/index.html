Common mistakes on Photographer resumes

  • Omitting specific photography niches (e.g., portrait, product) relevant to the job.
  • Failing to include technical details about equipment and software used.
  • Neglecting to quantify achievements or provide concrete examples of work.
  • Overlooking soft skills like client communication and adaptability.

FAQ

What technical skills should I highlight on a Photographer resume?

Focus on camera operation, lighting techniques, photo editing software, and specific photography niches like portrait or product photography.

How important are certifications for a Photographer resume?

Certifications like Certified Professional Photographer (CPP) or Adobe Certified Expert (ACE) can set you apart, but they're not mandatory. Highlight them if you have them.

Should I include my photography portfolio in my resume?

No, but include a link to your portfolio in your contact information or cover letter. Focus the resume on skills and experience.

How can I make my Photographer resume stand out?

Quantify achievements, showcase specific projects, and tailor your resume to the job description using relevant keywords.

What if I'm a beginner Photographer with limited experience?

Highlight personal projects, workshops, or volunteer work. Emphasize transferable skills like creativity and attention to detail.

Do I need to list every photography tool I've used?

List tools relevant to the job. For example, if applying for product photography, mention experience with studio lighting and photo editing software.
