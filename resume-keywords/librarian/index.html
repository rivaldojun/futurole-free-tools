Common mistakes on Librarian resumes

  • Omitting specific library software or systems used (e.g., Koha, SirsiDynix).
  • Failing to quantify achievements, such as increased patronage or budget savings.
  • Neglecting to highlight community engagement or instructional initiatives.
  • Overlooking certifications or professional development relevant to librarianship.

FAQ

What technical skills are most important for a Librarian resume?

Focus on cataloging systems (MARC, OCLC), integrated library systems (ILS), and digital resource management. Include metadata creation and familiarity with classification systems like Dewey Decimal.

How should I format my Librarian resume for ATS?

Use clear headings, bullet points, and standard fonts. Include keywords like 'collection development' and 'reader's advisory' in both the skills and experience sections.

Do I need certifications on my Librarian resume?

Certifications like Certified Librarianship (CL) or Digital Archives Specialist (DAS) can strengthen your resume, especially if required by the job posting.

How can I show impact in my Librarian resume?

Quantify achievements, such as 'Increased program attendance by [X]%' or 'Managed a $[X] budget with [X]% savings.'

Should I include soft skills on my Librarian resume?

Yes, soft skills like community engagement, instructional design, and problem-solving are highly valued in librarianship.

What if I lack experience in a specific library system?

Highlight transferable skills, such as database management or instructional technology, and express willingness to learn new systems.
