FAQ

What keywords should I include for a Supply Chain Analyst role?

Focus on terms that match the job description, such as demand forecasting, inventory optimization, and ERP. Include industry‑specific phrases like lean methodology and vendor management.

How do I highlight my analytical skills?

Show specific projects where you used data tools to solve supply chain problems. Include the tools and the outcome. Demonstrate how your analysis impacted the business.

Should I list all my certifications?

Only list certifications that are relevant to the role and that you have completed. Remove outdated or unrelated credentials. Keep the list concise and focused.
