Common mistakes on Growth Marketer resumes

  • Listing channels without stating budget scope, targeting strategy, or efficiency metrics achieved
  • Describing experiments without naming the hypothesis, methodology, statistical significance, or outcome
  • Claiming 'growth' ownership while only executing tactics owned by other teams (e.g., creative, engineering)
  • Using vanity metrics (impressions, followers, traffic) without connecting them to revenue or retention impact

FAQ

What are the most important Growth Marketer resume keywords for ATS?

ATS filters prioritize hard skills like A/B testing, SQL, Google Analytics 4, paid social platforms (Meta, TikTok), paid search, SEO, email automation tools (Braze, Iterable), attribution modeling, CRO, and mobile attribution (AppsFlyer, Adjust). Include specific channel names and tool names exactly as they appear in the job description.

How do I show growth experiment results on a resume without revealing confidential data?

Use relative improvements and anonymized scale indicators. State the hypothesis, the variable tested, the statistical confidence level, and the directional impact on a core metric like CAC, LTV, or retention rate. Replace absolute revenue figures with percentages or indexed baselines (e.g., 'improved trial-to-paid conversion by 15%').

Should a Growth Marketer resume emphasize breadth across channels or depth in one?

Match the job description. Early-stage roles typically demand T-shaped marketers with breadth across paid, owned, and earned channels plus SQL. Late-stage or specialized roles (e.g., 'Growth Lead, Paid Social') require documented depth, budget scale, and team leadership in that specific channel. Mirror the language of the posting.

What is the difference between a Growth Marketer and a Digital Marketer resume?

A Growth Marketer resume centers on the full funnel (acquisition through retention), experimentation velocity, unit economics (LTV:CAC, payback), and product collaboration. A Digital Marketer resume often focuses on top-of-funnel execution, brand awareness, campaign management, and channel-specific KPIs like CPM or CTR without the mandate to optimize the entire user lifecycle.

How technical should a Growth Marketer resume be?

Include SQL proficiency, experience with event-based analytics (Amplitude, Mixpanel), and familiarity with tracking implementation (GTM, Segment, server-side). If you write Python or R scripts for modeling or automation, list them. If you rely on no-code tools, name the specific platforms (Zapier, Retool, internal dashboards) and the data flows you manage.

Where do I put certifications on a Growth Marketer resume?

Place active platform certifications (Google Ads, Meta Blueprint, GAIQ) in a dedicated 'Certifications' section near the bottom or beside 'Technical Skills.' Reforge or CXL certificates signal advanced methodology knowledge. Do not list expired certifications or generic 'digital marketing' certificates that lack tool specificity.
