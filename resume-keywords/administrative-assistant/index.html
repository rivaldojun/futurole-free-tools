Common mistakes on Administrative Assistant resumes

  • Listing generic skills without context or examples
  • Using vague action verbs instead of specific accomplishments
  • Overloading the resume with irrelevant tasks or outdated software
  • Ignoring ATS formatting requirements like clear section headings and keyword usage

FAQ

How do I include keywords effectively in an Administrative Assistant resume?

Review the job description and include exact phrases and skills that match your experience. Use these keywords naturally in your summary and bullet points to pass ATS scans.

Should I list software skills even if I am not an expert?

Include software you use regularly and are comfortable with. Be honest about your skill level and consider gaining basic proficiency in common tools like Microsoft Office.

What certifications help improve my chances as an Administrative Assistant?

Certifications like CAP and Microsoft Office Specialist show your commitment and competence. They can distinguish you from other candidates and sometimes are requested by employers.
