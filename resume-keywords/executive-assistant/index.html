Common mistakes on Executive Assistant resumes

  • Listing daily tasks instead of measurable achievements.
  • Leaving out specific software tools like Salesforce or Asana.
  • Using passive phrases like assisted with or helped.
  • Ignoring industry-specific terminology.

FAQ

Should I include my typing speed?

Include it only if the job description requests it. Focus on your ability to manage executive priorities.

How do I show discretion on a resume?

Use phrases like handled confidential information. Describe the sensitive nature of the data you managed.

Do I need a specific degree for this role?

Employers often prefer a bachelor's degree. Your track record supporting C-suite leaders usually carries more weight.
