Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the match score?
The tool uses basic keyword matching and doesn't replace a human review, but it provides a good starting point for assessing your resume's fit.
Does this tool work with PDF resumes?
No, you'll need to paste the text content of your resume into the text box. We recommend copying and pasting the text from your PDF.
What if I don't see a good match score?
The missing requirements section will help you identify what to add or emphasize in your resume. You can also use our AI-powered resume tailoring service to optimize your document.
Is my data secure?
Yes, all processing happens in your browser and no data is sent to our servers. Your information never leaves your device.
