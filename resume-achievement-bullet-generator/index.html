What this tool does

Paste a single job duty (e.g., “Managed a team of sales reps”) and optionally specify the role you’re applying for. In under ten seconds you’ll receive three achievement‑focused bullet points, each containing metric placeholders ready for you to customise.

FAQ

How are the metric placeholders formatted?

Placeholders appear as [X%], [number] or [$ amount]. Replace them with the actual figures that reflect your achievements.

Can I use this tool for any industry?

Yes. The generator uses a broad set of action verbs and templates that work across most professional fields.

Is the generated text SEO‑friendly?

The bullets incorporate common resume keywords (e.g., “increased”, “streamlined”, “exceeded”) which help applicant tracking systems recognize relevant achievements.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser, so the tool works offline after the page loads.
