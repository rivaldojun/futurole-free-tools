Frequently Asked Questions

How does this tool work? +
Our tool compares text from your resume and job description using keyword matching. It highlights which requirements from the job are present in your resume and which are missing, helping you identify areas for improvement.
Can I use this tool for multiple jobs? +
Yes! You can compare your resume against multiple job descriptions by simply pasting each one into the tool. The results will show you which skills are relevant for each job.
How accurate is the matching? +
The matching is based on text similarity. While it's highly accurate for common keywords and phrases, it may not capture nuanced differences in job descriptions or resume formatting. For best results, review the detailed results provided.
What if my resume is in PDF? +
Currently, the tool only supports plain text inputs. If you have a PDF resume, you'll need to copy and paste the text content into the tool.
