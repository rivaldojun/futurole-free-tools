What is an ATS Keyword Checker?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by employers to manage job applications. Many ATS systems scan resumes for specific keywords from job descriptions before they reach a human reviewer. This tool helps you identify which keywords from your job description are missing from your resume, increasing your chances of passing the initial ATS scan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my resume being rejected by ATS?
ATS systems often reject resumes that don't contain the exact keywords from the job description. This tool helps you identify which keywords are missing so you can optimize your resume accordingly.
How do I know which keywords to include?
Copy the entire job description and paste it into this tool along with your resume. The tool will analyze both texts and identify keywords from the job description that are missing in your resume.
Is this tool 100% accurate?
While this tool provides a good estimate, ATS systems can be complex and may have additional requirements beyond simple keyword matching. However, this tool will help you catch most missing keywords.
Can I use this tool for any job application?
