Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cover letter hook? A cover letter hook is the first sentence or opening line of your cover letter. It should be attention-grabbing and relevant to the job, setting the tone for the rest of your application.

Why is the first sentence important? The first sentence is your chance to make a strong impression on the recruiter. If it's weak or generic, the recruiter may lose interest before reading the rest of your letter. A good hook increases the likelihood that your application will be read carefully.

How does this tool work? This tool uses a simple algorithm to combine the job title and your key skill into a coherent, compelling sentence. It's designed to help you quickly come up with a starting point for your cover letter, which you can then refine.
