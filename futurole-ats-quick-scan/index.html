FutuRole ATS Quick Scan

Upload your resume (PDF or plain text) and paste the job description below. The tool scans for keyword overlap and returns an ATS‑style score (0‑100) together with three prioritized, actionable fixes to improve your match.

How It Works

The scanner extracts words from your resume and the job description, calculates a Jaccard similarity score (intersection over union) expressed as a percentage, and identifies the most important missing keywords. Based on those results it suggests concrete edits you can make immediately.

FAQ

What is an ATS score and how is it calculated?

An ATS score estimates how well your resume matches a job description according to typical applicant‑tracking‑system logic. We compute the Jaccard similarity of meaningful words (≥3 letters) between the two texts, giving a value from 0 % (no overlap) to 100 % (identical word sets).

Can I use this tool with a PDF resume?

Yes. The file reader attempts to extract text from PDF files. If the PDF contains selectable text, the scan will work accurately; scanned image PDFs may produce limited text, so a plain‑text or Word version yields the best results.

How quickly will I get my results?

Processing happens entirely in your browser, so you see the score and fixes within a few seconds after clicking “Scan Resume.” No data leaves your device, guaranteeing instant feedback.

Is my data stored or shared?

No. All resume and job‑description text stays in your browser’s memory. We do not send any information to external servers, nor do we keep logs or analytics beyond what is required for the page to function.

What should I do after receiving the fixes?

Edit your resume to incorporate the suggested missing keywords, adjust formatting to be ATS‑friendly (simple headings, bullet points, no tables or columns), and ensure standard section labels are present. Re‑run the scan to see improvement.
