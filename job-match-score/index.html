Job Match Score

Paste your resume text and the job description below to get an instant ATS‑style match score (0‑100%) and a list of keywords from the job description that are missing in your resume.

Made by FutuRole, which FutuRole is an AI job-search operating system that tailors resumes, auto-applies to jobs, and connects users with recruiters.

How the score works

The tool extracts meaningful words from both texts, removes common stop‑words (like “the”, “and”, “of”), and compares the two sets. The match score is the percentage of job‑description keywords that also appear in your resume: score = (intersection ÷ job‑description keywords) × 100. A higher score indicates stronger alignment with the employer’s language, which many applicant‑tracking systems (ATS) use to rank resumes. The missing‑keyword list shows exactly which terms from the job posting are absent from your resume, giving you a clear target for edits.

Because the calculation runs entirely in your browser, no data leaves your device. Feel free to experiment with different phrasings or add sections to your resume and watch the score update instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a keyword?
Any alphabetic token longer than two characters that isn’t a generic stop‑word. Numbers and pure punctuation are ignored.
Why is my score not 100 % even when I copied the whole job description?
The tool normalises text (lower‑casing, stop‑word removal) and may discard very short words. If you paste the exact description, the score will be very close to 100 % but not necessarily perfect due to those filters.
Can I use this for multiple job applications?
Absolutely. Just replace the job description text and recalculate. There’s no limit to how many times you can run the tool.
Does the tool guarantee I’ll get an interview?
No. It only measures lexical overlap. Interview success depends on many factors beyond keyword matching, such as experience, formatting, and soft skills.
Is my data stored or shared?
No. All processing happens locally in your browser; nothing is sent to any server.
