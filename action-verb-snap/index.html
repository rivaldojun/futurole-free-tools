Action Verb Snap

How It Works

Action Verb Snap is a quick tool that helps you evaluate the strength of your resume by counting action verbs and suggesting stronger alternatives. By pasting your resume text, you get a verb count, a list of stronger verbs, and a quick note on verb variety. This helps you tailor your resume for ATS and recruiters, ensuring your achievements are highlighted with impact.

FAQ

What are action verbs?
Action verbs are words that describe activities or accomplishments, such as “managed,” “designed,” or “implemented.” They convey tangible results and help recruiters quickly assess your impact.
Why is verb variety important?
Using a wide range of strong verbs prevents repetition, keeps your resume engaging, and signals diverse skill sets. ATS systems also favor varied language, improving your chances of passing automated scans.
How does the tool determine stronger alternatives?
The tool contains a curated list of common action verbs and their more powerful counterparts. When a verb is detected, the script suggests a stronger replacement that retains the original meaning but adds impact.
Can I trust the suggestions?
While the suggestions are based on widely accepted best practices, you should review them in context. Some alternatives may not fit your specific experience, so use them as a starting point rather than a final replacement.
