How it works

This tool gives you a quick snapshot of the skills that matter for a role. Type a job title and you will see the most common requirements for that position. Paste your own skills comma-separated and the tool will match them to typical roles and highlight what is missing. It runs entirely in your browser, no data is sent anywhere, and results are instant.

Use the snapshot to prioritize learning, update your resume, or prepare for interviews. The output is a starting point, not a definitive assessment. Real hiring decisions depend on context, experience and how you demonstrate skills.

FAQ

Is my input stored?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I use this for a resume?

Yes. Use the top missing skills to guide which keywords and projects to highlight or develop next.

How accurate is the match?

The tool uses a curated set of common skills for typical roles. It is meant for fast orientation, not detailed job analysis.
